# NeuroForgeIO branding and attribution

RKC (Repository Knowledge Compiler) is an open-source project maintained by
**NeuroForgeIO** and its contributors. RKC-owned source code, documentation,
schemas, examples, and built-in plugins are released under the [MIT
License](../LICENSE).

Commercial products, hosted services, and derivatives are welcome. When
redistributing or presenting RKC-owned work, retain `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` and
credit **NeuroForgeIO and RKC contributors**. A short attribution such as
“Powered by RKC from NeuroForgeIO” is sufficient for a product or UI; the
license does not require a particular placement or branding treatment.

RKC does not relicense material it consumes. Go modules, `llama.cpp`, SCIP
indexers, model weights, generated indexes, and repository content retain the
licenses and attribution requirements of their respective owners. The exact
reviewed dependency and model boundaries are maintained in
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), the model lock, and the
generated SBOMs.

Generated atlases and NotebookLM packs describe their source repository's
license separately; they do not silently change that repository's terms. Keep
the generated provenance, citation, and attribution records when sharing an
export.
