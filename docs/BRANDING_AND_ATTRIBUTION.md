original RKC source code, documentation, schemas, examples, and built-in

Commercial products, hosted services, and derivatives are welcome under that

license, marking modified files, preserving applicable source notices, and
carrying the applicable contents of [`NOTICE`](../NOTICE). The NOTICE is
informational and does not add restrictions to the Apache-2.0 grant. Apache-2.0
does not grant general trademark rights beyond customary origin descriptions
and reproducing NOTICE content.

RKC does not relicense or claim ownership of material it consumes. Go modules,
`llama.cpp`, SCIP indexers, model weights, generated indexes, and repository
content retain the licenses and attribution requirements of their respective
owners. The exact reviewed dependency and model boundaries are maintained in
[`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`](../THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md), the model lock, and the
generated SBOMs.

Generated atlases and NotebookLM packs describe their source repository's
license separately; they do not silently change that repository's terms. Keep
the generated provenance, citation, and attribution records when sharing an
export.

## Standard first-party document footer

Every first-party Markdown document ends with the same short stewardship and
license footer below. Documentation validation enforces the exact terminal
footer so newly added guides, examples, governance documents, and architecture
records cannot omit the publisher, contributor-ownership, and public-use terms.

Generated RKC output, vendored or upstream license text under `LICENSES/`,
third-party/vendor trees, and the mixed-source `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`
inventory are explicitly excluded. Those materials retain their own generated
provenance or upstream terms and must not be rewritten as RKC-owned prose.
